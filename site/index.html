The CPCB AQI is a 24-hour average (CO and ozone: highest 8-hour average). After a short, intense spike — such as Diwali fireworks — it stays high for up to a day even when the air outside has already cleared, which is why the last-hour PM2.5 is shown next to each station. Sites that show an "instant" or US-scale AQI will give different numbers.
